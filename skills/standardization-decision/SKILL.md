# Standardization Decision

Resolve the ticket's normative question with the human. The result is a decision record, not draft standard prose.

## Build the decision tree

Map every material choice as a dependency tree. A question is material when a different answer could change feasibility, scope, normative meaning, evaluation, or a contradiction in the resulting standard.

The **frontier** is every material question whose prerequisites are settled. Ask the whole frontier in one round; defer dependent questions to later rounds.

Finding facts is agent work. Investigate discoverable facts or request a bounded research ticket. Ask the human for judgments, priorities, risk tolerance, and intended authority.

## Ask in rounds

Number each question and recommend an answer grounded in the available evidence:

```markdown
❓ **Q1** - **<decision title>**: <question, alternatives, consequences, and relevant uncertainty>

➡️ <recommended answer and why>
```

After each answer, recompute the tree. Surface minority evidence and credible alternatives instead of manufacturing consensus. If ambiguity can materially affect the outcome, keep it open or state what research would resolve it.

Stop when every remaining branch is either settled, recorded as uncertainty, or unable to materially change this ticket's resolution. Ask the human to confirm shared understanding before returning the result.

## Return the record

Provide:

- resolution
- rationale
- rejected alternatives and their trade-offs
- supporting and contradictory evidence
- uncertainty and assumptions
- supersession links when applicable
- follow-up research, decision, or task tickets now made expressible

The orchestrator records and resolves the ticket.
