# Standardization Research

Research facts; leave normative choices to decision work.

## Establish the contract

Require a bounded question, its expected effect on feasibility or a later decision, relevant source classes, and a stopping condition. If any is absent, sharpen the research ticket before searching.

Read [references/evidence-method.md](references/evidence-method.md) before gathering evidence.

## Deliver

Write one evidence artifact in the effort's evidence directory. Include:

- the bounded question and decision impact
- scope and source classes
- a compact coverage matrix
- findings with claim-level citations
- supporting and contradictory evidence
- uncertainty, omissions, and source limitations
- the stopping rationale
- implications and newly visible questions

Return the artifact path and a short synthesis to the orchestrator. Recommend research, decision, or task follow-ups when justified, but do not select the normative answer.
